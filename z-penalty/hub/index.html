Sound

Plays in game when a penalised stretch starts: a ramp climb by default, or the top of every jump too when Ramps only is off in the HUD customizer. At most one sound per half second. The panel itself (position, parts, colours) is set in Momentum Mod's HUD customizer, where it is listed as Z Penalty. A change here reaches a running game at the next map load.
