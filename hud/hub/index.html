What the kit's tools show in game.

Your Strafe Review is older than 1.9.0 and draws the jump-1 line itself. Since 1.9.0 that line is the First Jump HUD module, set up in the game's HUD customizer. Update Strafe Review on the Modules page and this switch goes away; until then it turns the old line on or off.
