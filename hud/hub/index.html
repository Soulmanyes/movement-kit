HUD

What the kit's tools show in game.

After jump 1 lands: landing speed, gain %, gain per strafe and the biggest mistake, at the top middle of the screen. Drawn by Strafe Review's recorder in Momentum Mod. Off by default, so it never covers your own HUD.
JHUD
After every jump in bhop: the jump's number, speed, speed change, gain % and sync, in the middle of the screen, coloured by how good it was. Its position, size, font, colours, fields and thresholds are set in Momentum Mod's own HUD customizer, where it is listed as JHUD, and saved with your HUD preset. Turn it off there too.
Patched game files
The game's HUD files with each tool's changes, in custom\0-movekit. Made again on every install and game start, from the copy the game would load otherwise.
