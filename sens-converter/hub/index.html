Sens Converter

Reference sens

A DPI, m_yaw and sensitivity to keep the horizontal speed of (0.022 is the game's default m_yaw). It opens on your game sens, turned into this DPI and m_yaw.

Your setup in Momentum

Your mouse's DPI, and the m_yaw and sensitivity Apply sets in the game. The sensitivity is worked out from the reference; change it and the reference follows.

Only the horizontal speed stays the same: m_pitch is left as it is, so vertical aim gets faster or slower with the new DPI and sens. The hub can't change your mouse's DPI: set it in your mouse's own software, and in Momentum's mouse settings so its cm/360 reads right.
