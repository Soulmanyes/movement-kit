Sound

A constant sound on your desktop for as long as you ride a surface in the air in Momentum Mod: a surf ramp or a slide down a slope, and a sloped ceiling (a headsurf), which sounds the same unless you separate it (then it can have its own sound, and a built-in one plays brighter). Each row picks a built-in sound, or uses a file or a recording of your own. It starts a couple of ticks after you touch the surface and stops when you leave it, land or slow down. A change here applies at once, also while the game runs.
Same as ramps
File… uses a sound of your own: a WAV (MP3, OGG and others too when ffmpeg is installed). Ramps and ceilings repeat it (1 to 15 s, a longer file is cut; its end blends into its start); a hit plays it once (up to 2 s, the silence before it trimmed). ● Rec records one from your microphone instead: it opens a window that says what that sound needs. It applies with Apply. Each sound has its own Volume %.
