Sound

A constant sound on your desktop for as long as you ride a surface in the air in Momentum Mod: a surf ramp or a slide down a slope, and a sloped ceiling (a headsurf), which plays a brighter version of the same sound. It starts a couple of ticks after you touch the surface and stops when you leave it, land or slow down. A change here applies at once, also while the game runs.
File… uses a sound of your own: a WAV (MP3, OGG and others too when ffmpeg is installed). Ramps and ceilings repeat it (1 to 15 s, a longer file is cut; its end blends into its start); a hit plays it once (up to 2 s, the silence before it trimmed). It applies with Apply.
