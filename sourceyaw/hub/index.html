Trainer sounds

Each mistake has its own sound: a constant tone that lasts while the mistake does, or a game sound. Pitch (Hz): the tone's note, lower number = lower note. Wait (ticks): how long the mistake must last before it sounds; one tick is 1/66 s or 1/100 s, so a higher wait lets short slips go.
Volume and repeat
Tone volume. A game sound plays again every this many seconds while the mistake lasts (0: once)
